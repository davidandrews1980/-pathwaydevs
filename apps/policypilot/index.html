What it does

PolicyPilot is for people who run a website or app and need the legal pages that go with it. You answer a short questionnaire about your business, the data you collect and the tools you use. Each answer turns into a clause in the document, so the result reflects what you told it instead of a template you have to rewrite.

It drafts privacy policies, terms of service, cookie policies, EULAs and return policies. Each document gets a hosted link and an embed snippet you can paste into your site or app footer. If your laws or your tools change, you regenerate the document and the hosted link updates.

  • Questionnaire-driven drafting, one answer per clause
  • Privacy policies, terms, cookie policies, EULAs and return policies
  • A hosted link plus an embed snippet for each document
  • Regenerate when your site or the rules change
  • You can start free

What it is for, and how it was built

What it is for: getting the standard legal pages onto a small site or app without starting from a blank page.

How it is made: a questionnaire that turns each answer into a clause, plus hosted document pages that can be regenerated. It is a Pathway Devs product, built and run by us, the same as the other apps on this page.

Good to know

PolicyPilot is a drafting tool. Not legal advice. Not reviewed by a licensed attorney.
