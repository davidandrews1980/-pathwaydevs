A privacy policy for the form on your website

When a visitor types their name, email, or phone number into your contact form, they're handing you personal information. A clear privacy policy linked near the form tells them what you'll do with it. Here's a simple way to get one in place.

What to sort out first

  1. Which fields your form asks for
  2. Where submissions go (inbox, CRM, email tool)
  3. A link to your privacy policy, placed by the submit button or in your footer
  4. What happens after someone submits

Why link a hosted page instead of pasting text into your site

One hosted page can serve every form, popup, and landing page you run. When you change tools or add a field, you update the policy in one place.

How PolicyPilot helps

Tell PolicyPilot about your business, the details your forms collect, and the tools that receive them. PolicyPilot generates and hosts the policy, giving you a link to add beside your form.
