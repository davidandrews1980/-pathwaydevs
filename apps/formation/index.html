What it does

Pathway Formation helps you set up an LLC. A wizard collects the state, the company name, the address, your registered agent and the members, then prepares the articles of organization. You pay the state what the state charges, and the desk files the paperwork.

The 50-state fee table is public and shows each state's filing fee, the name of the document, the agency and the typical processing time. Optional extras are shown as separate line items, and the desk quotes typical processing times from each state.

  • Step-by-step formation wizard
  • Public 50-state table of filing fees, documents and agencies
  • State fee passed through as its own line
  • Guides, plus a customer desk for your orders and documents
  • Takes real orders today

What it is for, and how it was built

What it is for: making company formation less opaque, with the state's fee visible up front.

How it is made: a wizard, a public 50-state fee table, guides, a customer desk and an operator queue, with orders stored on the server rather than in your browser. It is a Pathway Devs product, built and run by us.

Good to know

Pathway Formation prepares and files forms. Not a law firm. Not legal advice.

State fees change. Check the live fee table in the app before you rely on any number.
