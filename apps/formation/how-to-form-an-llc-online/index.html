How to form an LLC online

You can form an LLC online without a law firm. Your state wants a short set of facts and its filing fee. This page covers what to have ready and how Pathway Formation walks you through it.

What to have ready

  1. The state where you're forming
  2. Your LLC name, plus a backup in case the first is taken
  3. A business address
  4. A registered agent. You name your own.
  5. The names of your members

Steps

  1. Answer the wizard. State, name, address, registered agent, members.
  2. Pay at checkout. Payment runs through Stripe. The state fee is passed through at what your state charges, with no markup. Optional add-ons (such as EIN help, expedite, reminders, or the Command desk) are priced separately. Review your cart before you pay and keep only what you need.
  3. We file. Your packet goes to our filing desk, and you keep the documents.

What the state fee is

Every state sets its own fee. Michigan charges $50, Montana $35, and Massachusetts $500. We pass the fee through as-is. The state fee is one part of your total; any add-ons in your cart are charged on top of it.

Expedite moves your filing to the front of our queue. Each state sets its own processing time.
